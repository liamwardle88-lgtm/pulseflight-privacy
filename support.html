Short FAQ

How do I sign in?
You can use the app without signing in. Optional sign-in is with Google or X.

Why does the app ask for location?
Location is used only for Overhead alerts and Collecting someone, and only once you turn them on. You can deny permission and still use the rest of the app.

Why do I get notifications?
Optional flight alerts need notification permission. You can turn notifications off in your phone or browser settings.

What are Basic and PulseFlight Pro?
Basic is free. PulseFlight Pro is the paid tier. Pro is not on sale yet, so nothing will charge you until it launches; spotter tools on the Pro tab are free to try until then.

Is PulseFlight for flying or navigation?
No. It is not a cockpit display, not air traffic control, and not for navigation.

Where does the flight data come from?
Live aircraft positions come from adsb.lol. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
