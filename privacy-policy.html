PulseFlight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Operator: L. Wardle (United Kingdom)

PulseFlight (“the App”) is a flight-information companion. This policy explains what data we process and why. The developer, L. Wardle, is the data controller under UK data protection law (UK GDPR). The App is not for navigation or operational use.

1. Data we collect

All of these are optional. You can use the App without signing in or sharing your location.

  • Account details: if you sign in with Google or X, we receive your name, email address, profile picture link and your Google or X account ID. We use these to sign you in and manage your account.
  • Location (precise and approximate): only if you turn on overhead / nearby aircraft features and grant permission. When you are signed in with Overhead on, your location pin is stored on our server. Journal sightings may save the place you saw a plane. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Things you create: tracked flights and trips, Flying Journal entries and notes, badges, theme and display preferences. When you are signed in, these sync to our servers so they appear on your other devices.
  • Notification details: if you allow alerts, your phone or browser gives us a push subscription address and keys so we can send you flight alerts.
  • Sign-in session details: your IP address and browser / device type when you sign in, to keep your account secure.
  • Diagnostics: a short log of the background flight checks we run for you, and basic server logs (for example time and error codes), to keep the service working.
  • Device storage: the same data may be kept on your device for offline use.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not build advertising profiles.

2. How we use data

  • To provide tracking, boards, journal, overhead views and alerts you ask for
  • To sign you in, manage your account, and sync your data
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

  • Flight data (adsb.lol): to show aircraft near you, your location is sent to adsb.lol, our live flight data source.
  • Maps, place names and weather: to draw maps and name places, your device sends locations to OpenStreetMap, Overpass and BigDataCloud. Some cards fetch weather for a location from Open-Meteo through our server.
  • Service providers: hosting, sign-in, database and your phone’s or browser’s push notification service process data only on our instructions to run the App.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

These providers handle data under their own terms.

4. Security

All data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

5. How long we keep it

Account and synced app data are kept while your account is active. Overhead history is kept for about 14 days, and the background check log for about 2 days. You can remove tracked flights and journal entries in the App. Device data is under your control (clear app storage or uninstall).

6. PulseFlight Pro

PulseFlight Pro is the paid tier. This policy applies in the same way to Pro users.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use the App without signing in (data stays on the device)
  • Deny or remove location permission (overhead features will not use your location)
  • Turn off notifications in your phone or browser settings
  • Delete journal entries and tracked trips in the App
  • Delete your account or delete some of your data. We act on requests within one month.

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Contact

For privacy requests related to PulseFlight, email the developer (L. Wardle) at pulseflightdev@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
