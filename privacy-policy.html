PulseFlight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Operator: L. Wardle

PulseFlight (“the App”) is a flight-information companion. This policy explains what data we process and why. The App is not for navigation or operational use.

1. Data we process

  • Account data — if you sign in: email or provider account identifiers needed to authenticate you.
  • App data you create — tracked flights, Flying Journal entries, theme and display preferences. When signed in, these may sync to our servers so they appear on your other devices.
  • Device storage — the same data may be stored on your device for offline use.
  • Approximate location — only if you enable overhead / nearby aircraft features and grant permission. Used to show aircraft near you; not sold or used for advertising.
  • Technical logs — basic server logs (e.g. time, error codes) to keep the service running. We do not build advertising profiles.

2. Flight data from third parties

Live positions and related telemetry come from public ADS-B and similar sources when available. Schedule and airport reference data may come from public or licensed datasets. Those providers process data under their own terms.

3. How we use data

  • To provide tracking, boards, journal, and alerts you request
  • To sync your data when you are signed in
  • To maintain security and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Sharing

We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, authentication, database) who process data on our instructions. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Retention

Account and synced app data are kept while your account is active. You can remove tracked flights and journal entries in the App. Device data is under your control (clear app storage / uninstall).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your choices

  • Use the App without signing in (data stays on the device)
  • Deny location permission (overhead features will not use your location)
  • Delete journal entries and tracked trips in the App
  • Contact us to request account-related deletion

8. Contact

For privacy requests related to PulseFlight, email the developer (L. Wardle) at pulseflightdev@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
